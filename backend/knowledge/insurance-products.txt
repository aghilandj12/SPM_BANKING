Bancassurance Products Guide (v2.2)
The bank distributes insurance as a corporate agent of partner insurers; the insurer underwrites and settles claims.
Life Insurance (Term): cover typically 10-15x annual income; entry age 18-65; medical tests above ₹50 lakh cover or age 45.
Health Insurance: individual or family floater, sum insured ₹5 lakh to ₹50 lakh; 30-day initial waiting period, 2-4 years for pre-existing diseases; premiums eligible under Section 80D.
Home Insurance: structure and contents cover; recommended for all home loan customers, covering at least the outstanding loan.
Motor Insurance: comprehensive cover mandatory with vehicle loans.
Credit Protect: optional loan cover insurance clearing outstanding loan on death/disability.
Suitability: RM must complete a needs analysis; insurance cannot be made a condition for loan sanction except where mandated (e.g., hypothecated asset).
Free-look period: 30 days for life and health policies.
