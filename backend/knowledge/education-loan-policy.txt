Education Loan Policy (v1.8)
Coverage: Covers tuition, hostel, books, and travel for recognised institutions (domestic and international).
Eligibility: Indian national. Admission secured to higher education course.
Maximum loan amount: Up to ₹50,00,000 for domestic, ₹1,50,00,000 for international.
Collateral requirement: Tangible collateral (property/FD) required for amounts above ₹40,00,000.
Co-applicant requirement: Mandatory (parent/guardian/spouse) with proof of income.
Moratorium: Available until 6 months post course completion or 1 year after getting a job, whichever is earlier.
Interest servicing during moratorium: Simple interest applied.
