Personal Loan Policy (v2.1)
Facility type: Unsecured term loan.
Eligibility: Salaried applicants with minimum 1 year current employment or self-employed with 2 years IT returns.
Minimum income requirement: ₹30,000 per month.
Maximum tenure: 5 years (60 months).
Maximum loan amount: ₹15,00,000 or up to 10x monthly net income, whichever is lower.
Pricing: Risk-based on bureau score and existing exposure. Typical range 11.5% to 15.5%.
FOIR capped at 60% of net monthly income.
