Current Account Policy (v1.2)
Eligible entities: Proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, private/public limited companies, trusts, and self-employed professionals.
Minimum average quarterly balance (AQB): Standard ₹25,000; Business Plus ₹1,00,000; Premium Business ₹5,00,000.
Interest: Not payable on current account balances.
Free cash deposit limit: 10x AQB per month; charges apply beyond.
Overdraft: available only against a sanctioned limit (see Business Loan Policy) or FD lien.
Documents: entity constitution proof, GST registration or trade licence, PAN of entity, KYC of authorised signatories, board resolution (companies).
Cheque book, RTGS/NEFT, bulk payments and payroll services available.
