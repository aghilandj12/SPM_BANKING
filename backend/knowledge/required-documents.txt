General Required Documents

For Salaried Individuals:
- Identity Proof (Aadhaar, PAN)
- Address Proof (Utility bill, Aadhaar, Passport)
- Income Proof: Latest 3 months payslips, Form 16 for last 2 years.
- Bank Statements: Last 6 months salary account statement.

For Self-Employed Individuals:
- Identity/Address Proof
- Income Proof: Last 2 years IT Returns with computation of income, P&L, and Balance Sheet certified by a CA.
- Bank Statements: Last 6 months current account and savings account statements.

Product Specific Additional Documents:
Home Loan: Property title documents, agreement to sell, approved building plan, valuation report (if available).
Education Loan: Admission letter from institute, detailed fee structure.
Personal Loan: No additional documents usually required beyond standard KYC and income proof.

Accounts & Deposits:
Savings / Salary Account: Identity and address proof (KYC), PAN, recent photograph; salary account requires employer tie-up or salary slip.
Current Account: Entity constitution documents, GST registration or trade licence, entity PAN, KYC of authorised signatories.
Fixed / Recurring Deposit: Existing account with full KYC; Form 15G/15H for TDS exemption if applicable.

Other Loans:
Business Loan: 2 years audited financials and ITR, 12 months GST returns, 12 months current account statements, business proof.
Vehicle Loan: Proforma invoice from dealer, registration certificate and valuation report for used vehicles.
Gold Loan: KYC only up to ₹5 lakh; income proof above ₹5 lakh.

Cards, Insurance & Investments:
Credit Card: KYC, income proof (latest payslip or ITR); FD receipt for secured card.
Insurance: Proposal form, KYC, medical reports where required by insurer.
Investments: KYC (CKYC/KRA), completed risk-profile questionnaire, nominee details.
