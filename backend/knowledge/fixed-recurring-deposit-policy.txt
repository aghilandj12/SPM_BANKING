Fixed & Recurring Deposit Policy (v3.0)
Fixed Deposit (FD):
- Minimum deposit ₹10,000; tenure 7 days to 10 years.
- Card rates: 1 year 6.80%, 18 months 7.10%, 2-3 years 7.25%, 5 years 7.00% p.a. Senior citizens +0.50%.
- Interest compounded quarterly; payout monthly/quarterly or reinvested at maturity.
- Premature withdrawal permitted with 1.00% penalty on applicable rate; not allowed for Tax Saver FD (5 year lock-in, Section 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh).
- Auto-renewal for same tenure at prevailing rate unless instructed otherwise.
- Loan/overdraft against FD up to 90% of principal at FD rate + 1.00%.
Recurring Deposit (RD):
- Minimum installment ₹500 per month in multiples of ₹100; tenure 6 months to 10 years.
- Rates same as FD of equivalent tenure.
- Penalty ₹1.50 per ₹100 per month for delayed installment; 6 consecutive missed installments lead to premature closure.
TDS: 10% if interest exceeds ₹40,000 per year (₹50,000 for senior citizens), unless Form 15G/15H submitted.
Deposit insurance: covered by DICGC up to ₹5 lakh per depositor.
